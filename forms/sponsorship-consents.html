Form C · Team coordinator checklist

Consent Packet Submission Checklist

Before uploading, put all individually signed Form B sheets into one multi-page PDF (≤20 MB). Put signed Form A in a separate PDF (≤10 MB). Upload one video MP4 (≤45 MB).

  • ☐ Every adult appearing recognizably in the video has signed Form B.
  • ☐ Every minor appearing recognizably has a guardian-signed Form B and assent.
  • ☐ School has authorized use of campus, school identity, and necessary filming.
  • ☐ Principal/school head and coach signed Form A, if school-affiliated.
  • ☐ Responsible adult coach/coordinator and team representative signed Form A for other teams.
  • ☐ Original vertical video contains no identifiable unconsented bystanders or learner IDs.
  • ☐ No copyrighted songs, watermarks, private contact information, dangerous stunts, or AI impersonation.
  • ☐ Submission occurs during the approved entry window, Philippine Standard Time.
  • ☐ Coordinator keeps the originals of ALL signed documents for dispute verification.

List participants on a separate private roster for EBiZ verification if a release is missing. Do not include dates of birth, home addresses, medical data or government IDs.
