Video standards & eligibility

Consistent presentation protects participants and gives each team a fair opportunity.

  • Eligible: school, barangay, or formally organized community teams based in Region VIII (Eastern Visayas): Samar, Northern Samar, Eastern Samar, Leyte, Southern Leyte, or Biliran.
  • Applicable sports: basketball, volleyball, futsal, and other organized team sports with 6–18 named eligible team members. A roster above 18 is not fully covered.
  • Each team may submit one entry only, through an authorized adult (18+). No purchases, fees, or EBiZ accounts are required.
  • Video: 30–60 seconds, MP4, 9:16 portrait, minimum 720×1280, maximum 45 MB, clear voice and lighting. No watermarks, copyrighted music, AI-generated contestant impersonations, or misleading staging.
  • Suggested structure: 0–5 seconds team introduction; 5–20 who you are; 20–45 why you need support; 45–60 team commitment and closing.
  • Do not film children without consent, conduct dangerous stunts, capture active road traffic while riding, or expose contact details, school IDs, homes, or precise routines.
  • Filming on school premises or use of school names, seals, uniforms, and facilities requires permission from the competent school authority.
  • A school team needs principal/school head authorization plus coach/instructor endorsement; community teams need signed authorization from the responsible coach/coordinator. Every identifiable adult participant must sign a media consent; every identifiable minor must have parent/legal guardian consent and age-appropriate assent.

Official mechanics — proposed for DTI review

Prize and exclusions
One winning team receives up to 18 custom two-piece playing kits (jersey and shorts; equivalent sport-specific top/bottom where appropriate), with one approved design, player names and numbers, and an EBiZ sponsor mark. Maximum 18 sets in total; no extra sets, shoes, protective equipment, cash, transport, tournament registration, travel, or ongoing sponsorship. One delivery destination within Region VIII is included. The final prize valuation, material specifications, size chart, tax treatment, and published permit details must be fixed before launch.
Selection, transparency and anti-fraud
Up to eight eligible finalists are selected using uniform criteria. Final scores: 30% team story and goals, 25% demonstrated need/community involvement, 15% authenticity/presentation, and 30% verified public vote. Judges use documented rubrics and must disclose conflicts. One verified person may cast one official vote for one team during the voting period; duplicate, automated, purchased, incentivized or fraudulent votes are excluded after documented review. Facebook hearts, shares, comments and follower counts DO NOT count as official votes. Ties are resolved by higher judging subtotal, then higher story score, then a recorded neutral draw under any applicable DTI oversight. Voting opens only after all finalist entries are posted.
Acceptance, deadlines, award and complaints
Only one complete submission per distinct team before the server-enforced deadline. Corrections are accepted only before cutoff through the authorized official channel, without preferential extensions. Late, missing, incomplete, inauthentic, unsafe, or unconsented entries are rejected with documented reasons. Publication is subject to manual verification. The winning team is notified through its registered representative. Prize redemption is available for at least 60 days from winner notification/publication, whichever is later; documentation and roster approval are then required to start production. Allow a target 21 working days from final approved artwork, final sizes, and claim confirmation; deadlines for any particular sporting event are not guaranteed. Teams may send concerns through the EBiZ website; consumer rights and DTI jurisdiction are unaffected.
Rights and third-party platforms
Entries must be original and legally authorized. EBiZ receives a non-exclusive, royalty-free, time-limited license to review, trim, caption, format, and publish the approved submission on EBiZ channels for this challenge and related recap communications for up to 12 months after the winner announcement, subject to data-subject rights and withdrawal requests where applicable. No permission is given to synthesize participant faces/voices with AI or sell footage to third parties. Submitters must not include unlicensed music, brands, or identifiable bystanders. The promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Facebook or Instagram; participants release those platforms from promotion-related claims to the extent permitted by law. School/DepEd/EVRAA affiliation is not implied.
Restrictions and fairness
Residents/team members must satisfy the published eligibility criteria. EBiZ owners, employees, contest judges, contractors involved in judging, and immediate household members cannot compete. Paid or coordinated engagement manipulation and harassment lead to review and possible disqualification. Documented decisions are appealable through the sponsor's contact channel within three calendar days of notice, without waiving statutory complaint avenues. EBiZ cannot unilaterally alter material dates, prizes or mechanics once approved by DTI. No additional hidden criteria will be applied.

Privacy & child-safeguarding notice

Controller: EBiZ Solutions and Supplies, Brgy. Buray, Paranas, Samar, Philippines. Contact and privacy rights requests: official EBiZ contact page.

Purpose: eligibility verification, anti-fraud, consent checking, fair judging, authorized media publication, prize delivery, and lawful recordkeeping. Basis: participation and publication require informed and specific consents where applicable; certain minimal verification/recordkeeping may be supported by other lawful grounds. Marketing consent is optional and independent of contest eligibility.

Public reposts beyond EBiZ control may persist. Technical anti-abuse logs minimized and deleted within 30 days absent an incident. Final retention schedule must be reviewed before launch.
