Our Story

Crafted with Purpose

More than a printing company — we are a creative production partner built on craftsmanship, service, and community.

Established in 2009, EBiZ Solutions and Supplies has grown from a local printing provider in Samar into a trusted partner for businesses, schools, government agencies, sports teams, and organizations, delivering large-format printing, custom uniforms, full sublimation apparel, and cut-and-sew manufacturing solutions tailored to each client's needs.

"Every order carries a bigger purpose. When clients work with EBiZ, they are not only receiving professionally made products — they are also supporting a business model that strengthens households, empowers women, and helps create opportunity within local communities."

A significant part of our manufacturing is carried out by skilled stay-at-home mothers and local sewers, many working within or near their own barangays. We take pride in providing compensation above government minimum standards, because we believe dignified work deserves fair value.
